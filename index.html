Questions, answered.

Can a gaming router guarantee lower ping?

No. A router cannot change distance to a game server or your ISP's upstream route. NGU focuses on reducing avoidable local queue delay.

Can I use NGU behind my ISP modem or ONU?

Usually yes. The exact setup depends on your ISP device and whether you use bridge mode, DMZ or routed mode.
