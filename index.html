Questions, answered.

Can a gaming router guarantee lower ping?

No. A router cannot change the distance to a game server or your ISP's upstream route. NGU focuses on reducing avoidable local queue delay.

Can I use NGU behind my ISP modem or ONU?

Usually yes. The exact setup depends on your ISP device and whether you use bridge mode, DMZ or routed mode.

Do you sell the routers with gaming configuration?

Yes — this site is structured so each catalog item can later connect to your real stock, pricing, configuration and ordering workflow.
